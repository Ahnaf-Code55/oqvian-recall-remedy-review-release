# Attribution and scope

## U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC)

Retained recall notice text is credited to the U.S. CPSC. Official source URLs and retrieval dates are in `application/data/notices.json`. CPSC's current reuse notice says that website text and recall notices may be freely copied and distributed, requests CPSC credit, and prohibits implying CPSC endorsement. Recall photographs may be copied with recall notices; other site images may have separate rights. This source snapshot contains no product photographs.

## Constructed histories

The example histories are team-authored constructed fixtures. They are not real owner records, consumer complaints, customer cases, or independent evaluation labels. York's structured API row is a constructed stand-in where the source comparison marks it as such.
