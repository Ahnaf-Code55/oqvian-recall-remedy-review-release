# Retained notice data

This application includes four dated U.S. CPSC notice snapshots (IDs 8374, 9982, 974 and 10896), their selected source sections, hashes, and three constructed demonstration case families. Retrieval dates and official URLs are in `notices.json`; these files are historical snapshots, not proof of present-day notice status.

CPSC asks distributors to credit the agency and not imply endorsement. See the root `NOTICE` and `ATTRIBUTION.md`. No product photographs or personal owner records are included. Team annotations are interpretations, not adjudicated ground truth.
